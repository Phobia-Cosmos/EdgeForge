# EEG data perturbations for a controlled LoP study

Data perturbations can make a continuous-learning protocol harder, but a
lower accuracy after perturbation is not automatically Loss of Plasticity
(LoP). The perturbation must be label-preserving, its signal quality must be
measured, and the same fresh/warm fixed-budget counterfactual must be rerun.

## Safe experimental ladder

Use the original ISRUC medium subset as the immutable clean control. Derive
each condition under a new directory and record a manifest digest.

1. **Acquisition-gain calibration:** scale each subject by one scalar to the
   source-subject median RMS. This removes a nuisance difference without
   changing temporal shape or spectral proportions.
2. **Mild sensor noise:** add deterministic 0.5--30 Hz noise at SNR 20 dB to
   target epochs only. This approximates a modest sensor-domain shift while
   keeping labels and epoch boundaries unchanged.
3. **Slow gain drift:** multiply each target epoch by a smooth +/-10% envelope over its 30-second duration. This models electrode/amplifier gain drift while preserving channel order and waveform topology.
4. **Dose scan:** if the first conditions do not change the gate, increase one nuisance at a time (for example SNR 15/10 dB or a small channel cross-talk matrix). Do not combine doses until the single-dose utility checks pass.

## Utility gates

Before interpreting learning curves, require: labels byte-identical to the
clean set; epoch count and boundaries unchanged; no NaN/Inf or clipping;
file-level waveform correlation preferably above 0.98; perturbation SNR at the
requested dose; and no large unintended change in 0.5--40 Hz band fractions.
The manifest from `prepare-eeg-lop-conditions.py` stores RMS ratios,
correlations and perturbation RMS for every file. The visualization command
shows the transformed waveforms, spectra and RMS heatmap.

## LoP decision

For every dose, keep the source training recipe, target order, optimizer,
adaptation batches, held-out split, budgets and at least three independent
seeds fixed. Compare `fresh_gap = accuracy_fresh − accuracy_warm` at each
transition. A positive mean or a single difficult target is not enough: the
strict gate requires the direction to be supported across all transitions and
seeds with the seed-cluster bootstrap check. Also report retention and the
train/evaluation gap so domain-shift degradation is not mislabeled as LoP.

The current TCN three-seed pilot found that RMS equalization and target SNR-20
noise increased average fresh-gap at budgets 25/50, but both remained
mixed-direction and `blocked-inconsistent-direction`. Thus the safe data
modifications changed transfer sensitivity; they did not establish natural
LoP. The synthetic encoder-gradient lesion remains the separate positive
control for validating the gate.

The follow-up 10-epoch source-training + RMS-equalized TCN run was also
mixed-direction at budgets 25 and 50. Its checkpoint diagnostics showed no
monotonic warm embedding-rank collapse (mean effective-rank 3.09 at budget 0
and 2.99 at budget 50), so the extra source training did not turn acquisition
scale shift into a stable LoP signal.

The additional `target_gain_drift10` condition is generated with the same script. It is intentionally evaluated separately from additive noise so that an apparent dose effect cannot be attributed to two simultaneous nuisances.
